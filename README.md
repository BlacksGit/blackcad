# Black CAD

Design, sculpt, and slice 3D prints in one lightweight app. Includes a built-in slicer, printer
monitor, and an optional AI helper.

## Download

Get the newest version from [Releases](../../releases/latest):

- **Windows:** `BlackCAD-Setup-<version>.exe`. Installs for your user account only, with no admin prompt.
- **Android:** `blackcad-lite.apk`. Allow installs from your browser when your phone asks.

Black CAD updates itself. Open Preferences > Updates, or wait for the note after start-up.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Each release lists SHA-256 checksums so you can check your download.

## Plans

7-day free trial, then a monthly or longer plan. The app works offline until the plan's end date.
